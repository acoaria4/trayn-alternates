# A world of light

Rebuild of Radiance v03 after the first direction was rejected for resembling v01.

An impossible ivory landscape. A monumental gold sun portal. A Radiance tin suspended in the middle of it. Oversized typography lives in the same scene as the product, instead of a conventional split hero. Cream, pale latte, honey and champagne remain the palette. Only the firefly chapter turns espresso. Manrope 500/600 fills the screen; original Radiance lettering remains the identity.

Sequence: immersive floating-product hero → scroll-reading manifesto → two full-viewport image scenes (floating latte and sunburst macro) → enormous typographic bridge → dusk and the firefly sun → native FAQ → billboard-scale closing link. The swarm accompanies the whole journey, starting as sparse honey-gold motes around the hero tin. The repeated floating-tin gallery scene is removed. The desktop pin now spans 1.1 viewport widths rather than 2.25.

## Motion and assets

GSAP owns the intro, reading progression and desktop horizontal pair. Lenis is the sole smooth-scroll engine, already installed and connected to ScrollTrigger. Locomotive would add a redundant engine. Touch uses native vertical chapters. Reduced motion presents a static vertical story; all content exists before enhancement. A three-pixel honey-gold line at the top of the page shows total reading progress. This variant's native scrollbar is hidden only after enhancement; wheel, touch and keyboard scrolling remain native inputs.

Three.js is unnecessary: the spatial concept uses original rendered imagery, a transparent product layer, damped pointer parallax and scroll-linked transforms. One fixed, pointer-transparent Canvas holds the same original firefly swarm throughout the page. Nearby lights gently approach a fine pointer, linger, then return to autonomous wandering. Controls are excluded from attraction. Only actual text lines soften the lights; the reading chapters retain 24–44 visible amber fireflies instead of fading an entire text block. Other daylight scenes show 18–34 lights, and dusk gradually reveals the rest of the 40–100 particle pool with brighter cores and paired wings. A click, tap or Make a wish button sends a soft ripple of twelve glints through the dark chapter and briefly gathers an orbit. Gather/release forms twelve sunburst rays. A persistent pause control is available from the opening screen, touch uses autonomous motion, reduced motion is still, and background tabs suspend animation. No new animation dependencies or artwork are required.

Hero navigation is deliberately clear: an espresso Explore button with cream lettering. Five scattered supporting labels are removed, leaving the main typography, tin and entry link. Product-development disclosures remain in the reading chapter and footer.

Masters and prompts: docs/radiance/assets/v03/world. Delivery: radiance-v03/assets/world. Product documents remain shared. No invented nutrition claims, testimonials, purchase flows or metrics.
