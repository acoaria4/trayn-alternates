# A world of light

Rebuild of Radiance v03 after the first direction was rejected for resembling v01.

An impossible ivory landscape. A monumental gold sun portal. A Radiance tin suspended in the middle of it. Oversized typography lives in the same scene as the product, instead of a conventional split hero. Cream, pale latte, honey and champagne remain the palette. Only the firefly chapter turns espresso. Manrope 500/600 fills the screen; original Radiance lettering remains the identity.

Sequence: immersive floating-product hero → scroll-reading manifesto → full-viewport horizontal product triptych → enormous typographic bridge → interactive firefly sun → native FAQ → billboard-scale closing link.

## Reference evidence

Revisited the complete https://www.elevateonex.com/home progression and /contact in the browser on 29 October 2026. Observed huge type over full-screen media, word-by-word tonal progression, held services scenes, large media frames and oversized closing typography. The fish form a coordinated living scene with far more presence than background dust.

These principles inform hierarchy and choreography. No source assets, wording, identity or code are reused. Radiance's surreal environments, floating pack and firefly sun are original.

## Motion and assets

GSAP owns the intro, reading progression and desktop horizontal triptych. Lenis is the sole smooth-scroll engine, already installed and connected to ScrollTrigger. Locomotive would add a redundant engine. Touch uses native vertical chapters. Reduced motion presents a static vertical story; all content exists before enhancement.

Three.js is unnecessary: the spatial concept uses original rendered imagery, a transparent product layer, damped pointer parallax and scroll-linked transforms. Canvas has one responsibility: the original firefly simulation, with a gather/release sun formation, pause/static controls, and offscreen/hidden suspension.

Masters and prompts: docs/radiance/assets/v03/world. Delivery: radiance-v03/assets/world. Product documents remain shared. No invented nutrition claims, testimonials, purchase flows or metrics.
